### 4. Third-party datasets, media and recorded model outputs

The notebooks bring datasets, documents, images and recorded model responses with them.

- **Committed files.** Where a notebook reads committed files, they sit in a `data/` folder next to
  it, and that folder's `README.md` lists every file with its source and, where one is declared, its
  licence. Some material is redistributed on the basis of
  a restricted or unverified rights position, documented there file by file.
- **Downloaded files.** Material a notebook downloads at run time — annual reports, claim photos,
  data sets — lands in a git-ignored `_local/` folder next to it and is not redistributed here; the
  weights of the open models in `01_llm_basics` and `02_llm_techniques` go to the Hugging Face cache of
  the machine instead. Where a lesson needs it, the saved outputs quote from this material: Case Study 2
  prints the report passages it retrieved, and Case Study 3 shows twelve claim photos as thumbnails of
  at most 256 pixels.
- **Recorded model responses.** The recordings in the `data/` folders were generated by third-party
  models under those providers' terms of use. They are published so that the notebooks can be read,
  and partly run, without a key.
- **Slides.** The PDF in [`slides/`](slides/) quotes third-party material with its source: crops from
  insurers' annual reports, screenshots of web pages, apps and videos, photos from the Case Study 3 data
  set, and the logos and images on the speaker slide. Model providers are named, not shown with their
  logos.
